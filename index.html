Everyone who enters on a day gets the same rate; the more entries a day sells, the fewer PAMP each one receives. Buy more than 1000 by sending another transaction.
How the $FUEL amount is priced
The $FUEL amount follows its market price: if $FUEL drops 90%, an entry takes ten times as much of it. The price is the FUEL/WPLS PulseX pair's spot price, floored by a 15-minute average the auction builds from its own checkpoints of the pair: a drop is charged at once, a price pushed up for a few blocks does not make entries cheaper, and entries never pause for a price move. Each entry can only take what it costs when it lands, capped by the amount you saw. The contract logs the price it charged on chain, at most once every 10 seconds.

Lock for 7 to 1,000 days. Unstaking before the unlock date costs a penalty that grows the less time you served. There is a 14-day grace period after unlock, then a late penalty starts, scaling over 700 days. Every penalty is recycled into the reward pool for stakers still in — which is where all PAMP rewards come from; there is no fixed rate. Staked PAMP also earns the protocol's PLS yield (20% of every entry, shared with unclaimed PAMP), claimable at any time without touching the stake and paid in full on unstake. Daily drip: —
The pool drips its daily rate — 1% at launch, adjustable between 1% and 15% by a rate setter fixed at deployment — in 144 ten-minute intervals; an interval nobody burns stacks onto the next call. Each burn buys $PAMP from the PAMP/WPLS PulseX pair, refuses anything under 90% of the pair's 15-minute average, and sends the tokens to 0x…dEaD. It is a port of the $FUEL protocol's buy-and-burn with the owner removed. Until the pair exists the PLS simply accumulates.

Emission curve

Loading the emission curve…0%
A steep first fortnight, then geometric decay: days 1 to 14 fall so that day 14 emits half of day 1, and from there every day emits 0.997698× the day before, down to a tenth of day 14 by day 1000. The amounts are fixed on chain and, with day 0 minted at genesis, sum to exactly 10,000,000 PAMP. A day nobody enters emits nothing at all.
